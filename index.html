How it works

From inbox to action in three steps

We deploy a self-hosted automation pipeline connected to your email. You own the data, the logic, and the infrastructure.

01

Connect your email

Gmail, Outlook, or any IMAP provider. We configure triggers filtered by sender, subject, or label — only relevant messages enter the pipeline.

02

AI scores and classifies

An LLM agent reads the email content (body or attachments), evaluates it against your custom rules, and returns structured data — scores, categories, extracted fields.

03

Results go where you need them

Telegram alerts, Slack messages, Google Sheets rows, CRM entries, or any API endpoint. Formatted, filtered, and delivered — no context switching required.
